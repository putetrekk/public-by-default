why.txt
  • Discoverable. The next person can find the information through search (and maybe add to it).
  • The right person answers. You don't need to know exactly who knows best.
  • No telephone game. Invite people to the thread instead of explaining from scratch four times.
  • No shadow work. The team sees what's pulling people away from what they planned.
  • Keep the entire team informed. Works across floors, cities and even working from home!
  • Start off on right foot. The longer a DM gets, the tougher it is to move it anywhere else.
but...
"I know who knows the answer, why should i bother the rest of their team?"
Tag them in the channel post! You'll still get your answer, and everyone else gets it for free. If they're away, busy or wrong, someone else can step in.
"I think my issue might be too basic to discuss in front of everyone."
Is it just an irrational fear, or does your workplace ridicule well-intended inquiries? If it's the former, you might just have the opportunity to break a similar barrier for someone else.
"If DMs are so useless, why do they exist at all?"
There are of course exceptions. HR issues, private conversations, planned surprises: DM away.

Public channels spread knowledge like wildfire. Use them!
